# BoliCheck demo and submission

## Record a 2 minute 20 second demo

Use **Gnani speech** mode and your own voice in a fictional loading scenario. Keep the transcript and the Gnani model label visible. The following timing is a script, so adjust your narration to what the app actually returns.

| Time | Show | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Point to the 24 expected crates. | “This load needs 24 blue crates. I’m going to report 14, the number we actually counted.” |
| 0:15–0:43 | Record “Blue crates चौदह हैं.” Show the heard count, shortage and disabled confirmation. | “Gnani Prisma transcribed the Hinglish clip. BoliCheck compared 14 with the manifest and flagged 10 short. The handoff is on hold.” |
| 0:43–1:03 | Record “चौदह नहीं, चौबीस.” Show the recount prompt if both numbers appear. | “This clip has a correction. When both values appear in the transcript, BoliCheck asks for one final count.” |
| 1:03–1:36 | Record “Blue crates चौबीस हैं,” then select cartons and record “Sealed cartons बारह हैं.” | “The recount matches the crates. The cartons also match. I check the counts and confirm handoff.” |
| 1:36–2:05 | Show confirmed handoff and explain the pipeline. | “Prisma does the speech recognition. BoliCheck keeps the planned counts local and applies the same reconciliation rules to each item. The supervisor reviews what the model heard.” |
| 2:05–2:20 | End on the confirmed screen or show your observed audio results. | “The demo uses a synthetic load. I’m measuring whether the system reaches the right dispatch decision from a spoken count.” |

If Prisma normalizes the correction down to one number, try “चौदह या चौबीस” for the recount case. Show the actual output. An unclear or incorrectly transcribed count is useful evidence when you explain its effect on the decision.

## Check real speech before recording

Read the three crate lines above once in a quiet room and once with background noise from a fan or a recorded sound with no identifiable voices. Also record the carton count so you can complete the handoff. For each run, log the spoken quantity or ambiguity, what Prisma returned, and the app decision. Report the number of correct **decisions** out of the number of clips tried. Call this a small demo check. A fan in the room does not simulate telephony.

For the public clip, open with 24 expected, 14 heard and the held handoff. If targeting **Demo Day Drop**, make a separate 60-second cut. The submission form still asks for a 2–3 minute video.
